1) Ignore unless it repeats

Ignoring such behavior is inappropriate because influencing a witness directly affects the fairness of the trial and the integrity of the judicial process.

2) Hold the lawyer in contempt

This is the correct action. Influencing a witness is professional misconduct and obstruction of justice. A judge has the authority to hold the lawyer in contempt of court to maintain fairness and discipline in proceedings.

Permanently adjourning the case is not a proper response. The issue should be addressed by disciplinary action against the lawyer, not by stopping the case entirely.

Question No. 27General Awareness (Q.2)
Which of the following witness types is formally recognized in the BSA, 2023?
A)
Political representatives
B)
Character witnesses only
C)
Mythological witnesses
D)
Expert witnesses such as forensic analysts  ✓ (Correct Answer)
Correct Option: D) Expert witnesses such as forensic analysts
Explanation:

The correct answer is Expert witnesses such as forensic analysts.

📌 Key Points

  • Expert witnesses are individuals with specialized knowledge, skills, or expertise in a particular field relevant to a legal case. They are formally recognized under the BSA (Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam), 2023.
  • The role of expert witnesses includes providing technical, scientific, or professional opinions to help the court understand complex evidence or facts.
  • Fields of expertise commonly considered include forensics, medicine, ballistics, engineering, accounting, and digital forensics.
  • The BSA, 2023 highlights the importance of expert opinions in cases involving scientific analysis, forensic evidence, and technical data.
  • Unlike lay witnesses, expert witnesses are allowed to offer opinions based on their expertise rather than limiting their testimony to factual observations. This makes their input critical in modern legal proceedings.

📌 Additional Information

  • Types of Witnesses:
    • Fact Witnesses: These witnesses testify about events or facts they have directly observed or experienced.
    • Character Witnesses: These individuals provide testimony about the character, reputation, or behavior of a person involved in the case. However, they are not involved in providing technical or scientific opinions.
    • Expert Witnesses: Recognized under the BSA, 2023, they provide specialized knowledge or analysis in areas like medical forensics, ballistics, handwriting analysis, and digital technology.
