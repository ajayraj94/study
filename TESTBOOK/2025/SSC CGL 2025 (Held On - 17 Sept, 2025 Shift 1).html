Explanation:
📌 Shortcut Trick

Convert the percentage into a fraction: 25% = 1/4.

Here, 1 unit (the numerator) represents the given value, and 4 units (the denominator) represents the original number.

Since 1 unit = 15, then 4 units = 15 × 4 = 60.

∴ The correct answer is 60.

Alternate Method

Given:

25% of a certain number = 15

Formula Used:

Number = (Given Value ÷ Percentage Rate) × 100

⇒ Let the original number be x.

⇒ According to the question, 25% of x = 15

⇒ (25 ÷ 100) × x = 15

⇒ (1 ÷ 4) × x = 15

⇒ x = 15 × 4

⇒ x = 60

∴ The correct answer is 60.

📌 Additional Information

Percentage to Fraction Conversion

Standard conversions: 25% = 1/4, 50% = 1/2, 75% = 3/4, 20% = 1/5, and 10% = 1/10.

Reverse Percentage Calculation

To find the original value when x% is given as y, use the formula: Original Value = (y × 100) ÷ x.

Base Change Property

If A is 25% of B, then B is (100 ÷ 25) × 100 = 400% of A.

  • The tomb reflects a unique blend of Hindu, Islamic, Jain, and Buddhist architectural styles, showcasing Akbar's policy of religious tolerance.
