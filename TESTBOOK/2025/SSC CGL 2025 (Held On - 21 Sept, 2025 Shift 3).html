📌 Key Points

  • Neither the Chola dynasty nor the Chera dynasty played any role in the establishment of the Bahmani kingdom, as their reigns predated the formation of this kingdom by several centuries.
  • The Bahmani kingdom was located in the Deccan region and was known for its distinct Indo-Islamic culture, architecture, and administrative systems.
  • The establishment of the Bahmani kingdom marked the beginning of an era of Islamic dominance in the Deccan region, which later saw the rise of other prominent kingdoms like the Bijapur Sultanate and the Golconda Sultanate.

📌 Additional Information

  • Chola dynasty:
    • The Chola dynasty was one of the longest-ruling dynasties in Indian history, spanning from the late 9th century to the 13th century CE.
    • They are renowned for their contributions to Tamil literature, the construction of grand temples like the Brihadeeswarar Temple in Thanjavur, and their extensive naval expeditions to Southeast Asia.
    • The Cholas played a significant role in the promotion of Hindu culture and the establishment of a sophisticated administrative system.
  • Chera dynasty:
    • The Chera dynasty was one of the ancient Tamil dynasties that ruled parts of present-day Kerala and Tamil Nadu.

    • It was deeply rooted in Hindu culture and had no connection to the Islamic Bahmani kingdom.

📌 Important Points

  • Geographical distinction: The Chola and Chera dynasties were centered in the Tamil region, whereas the Bahmani kingdom was established in the Deccan region of southern India.
  • Chronological difference: The Chola and Chera dynasties flourished several centuries before the Bahmani kingdom emerged.
  • Religious and cultural contrast: The Cholas and Cheras were deeply rooted in Hindu traditions, whereas the Bahmani kingdom represented Islamic governance and culture.

Question No. 28General Awareness (Q.3)
Which government scheme aims to provide financial support to senior citizens who may have lost their means of sustenance?
A)
Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)  ✓ (Correct Answer)
B)
Atal Pension Yojana (APY)
C)
Pradhan Mantri Vaya Vandana Yojana (PMVVY)
D)
National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP)
Correct Option: A) Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)
Explanation:

The correct answer is Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS).

📌 Key Points

  • The Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS) is a component of the National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP), launched by the Government of India.
  • It was introduced in 1995 and revised in 2007 under the Ministry of Rural Development.
  • The scheme provides a monthly pension to senior citizens aged 60 years and above who belong to families living below the poverty line (BPL).
  • The amount of pension varies depending on the beneficiary's age:
    • 60–79 years: ₹200 per month from the central government (states can top up).
    • 80 years and above: ₹500 per month from the central government.
  • IGNOAPS aims to provide social security and ensure the financial well-being of economically weaker sections of senior citizens.

📌 Additional Information

  • Atal Pension Yojana (APY):
    • Launched in 2015, it is focused on providing a guaranteed monthly pension to workers in the unorganized sector.
    • The entry age is 18–40 years, and the pension amount ranges from ₹1,000 to ₹5,000, depending on contributions.
    • It is co-contributory, with the government adding 50% of the contribution or ₹1,000 per year (whichever is lower) for eligible subscribers.
  • Pradhan Mantri Vaya Vandana Yojana (PMVVY):
    • Implemented by the LIC of India, this scheme aims to provide pension security to senior citizens aged 60 years and above.
    • It guarantees a fixed interest rate of 7.4% per annum for 10 years, payable monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, or yearly.
    • The maximum purchase price under the scheme is ₹15 lakh.
  • National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP):
    • Launched in 1995, it is an umbrella scheme aimed at providing social security to the poor, including old age pension, widow pension, and disability pension.
    • IGNOAPS is one of its key components, specifically targeted at senior citizens.
  • Key Differences:
    • IGNOAPS: Focuses on senior citizens in BPL families, providing small pensions.
    • APY: Caters to unorganized workers, offering a range of pensions based on contributions.
    • PMVVY: A fixed-income scheme for senior citizens with higher financial security.

📌 Important Points

  • IGNOAPS Implementation:
