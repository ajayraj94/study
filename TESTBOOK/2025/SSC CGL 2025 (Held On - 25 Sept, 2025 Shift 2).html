Question No. 26General Awareness (Q.1)
Which of the following refers to the extent to which demand for a particular commodity decreases because others are also consuming the same commodity?
A)
Snob effect  ✓ (Correct Answer)
B)
Demonstration effect
C)
Bandwagon effect
D)
Veblen effect
Correct Option: A) Snob effect
Explanation:

The correct answer is Snob Effect.

📌 Key Points

  • Snob Effect: This phenomenon occurs when the demand for a commodity decreases as more people start consuming it, reflecting a desire for exclusivity and uniqueness.
  • Consumer Behavior: Individuals influenced by the Snob Effect often prefer goods that are rare or limited in availability, as these signify exclusivity.
  • Contrast with Bandwagon Effect: Unlike the Bandwagon Effect, which thrives on mass adoption and conformity, the Snob Effect is driven by the need to stand out and avoid commonality.
  • Luxury Goods Market: The Snob Effect is most commonly observed in markets for luxury items, where exclusivity and rarity enhance appeal.
  • Conspicuous Consumption: This concept is closely linked to the economic principle of conspicuous consumption, where individuals showcase wealth and status through unique possessions.

📌 Additional Information

  • Demonstration Effect:
    • Also known as the "showcase effect," it represents how individuals mimic the consumption behaviors of others, particularly those of higher socio-economic status.
    • It is prevalent in developing economies, where exposure to new lifestyles through media and globalization influences purchasing patterns.
    • For example, aspiring consumers in rural areas may imitate urban consumption habits, aiming to elevate their social status.
  • Bandwagon Effect:
    • This effect describes a phenomenon where people adopt certain behaviors, preferences, or trends because others are doing the same.
    • It is widely observed in fashion, social movements, and marketing campaigns emphasizing product popularity.
    • For example, a sudden surge in demand for a trending clothing item due to celebrity endorsements showcases the Bandwagon Effect.
  • Veblen Effect:
    • Named after economist Thorstein Veblen, this effect explains why consumers perceive higher-priced goods as more desirable.
    • It is rooted in the idea that higher prices signal exclusivity, wealth, and prestige.
    • Luxury brands such as Rolex and Gucci often leverage the Veblen Effect to position their products as status symbols.
  • Applications of Snob Effect:
    • It is frequently observed in industries such as high-end fashion, exotic automobiles, and limited-edition collectibles.
    • Brands use exclusivity strategies like limited releases and high pricing to appeal to snob consumers.
    • For instance, the launch of limited-edition sneakers by brands like Nike or Adidas caters to consumers influenced by the Snob Effect.

📌 Important Points

  • Key Differences:
    • Snob Effect focuses on exclusivity and rarity, whereas the Bandwagon Effect thrives on mass adoption.
    • Demonstration Effect is influenced by socio-economic aspirations, while the Veblen Effect is tied to high prices and status signaling.
  • Economic Implications:
    • The Snob Effect can impact pricing strategies, as companies may intentionally limit supply to enhance perceived exclusivity.
    • It also highlights the importance of understanding consumer psychology in luxury goods marketing.

  • Satellite data also contributes to drought monitoring by analyzing vegetation health, soil moisture levels, and rainfall patterns, allowing for early intervention in affected areas.

    • The Cartosat series provides high-resolution imagery for mapping disaster-hit areas and planning relief measures.
  • Importance of Real-time Data:
    • Real-time data allows governments to issue early warnings, reducing the impact of disasters.
    • It enables quick allocation of resources to affected regions, such as food, water, and medical aid.

    • ISRO’s Disaster Management Support (DMS) Programme provides critical satellite-based services to support disaster management.
  • Global Systems:
    • International systems like the Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) and Copernicus Programme assist in disaster monitoring globally.

📌 Important Points

  • Drought Assessment: Vegetation indices derived from satellite data can indicate crop stress and help monitor drought conditions.
  • Technological Advancement: The use of remote sensing, GIS (Geographical Information Systems), and AI (Artificial Intelligence) has significantly enhanced disaster management capabilities in India.
