A)
10  ✓ (Correct Answer)
B)
8
C)
12
D)
9
Correct Option: A) 10
Explanation:
📌 Shortcut Trick

Current ratio (Man:Daughter) = 4 : 1 (Difference = 3 units)

Ratio after 20 years = 2 : 1. Make differences equal: multiply by 3 → 6 : 3

Increase in units = 6 − 4 = 2 units. These 2 units = 20 years.

1 unit = 10 years. Daughter's current age (1 unit) = 10 years.

∴ The correct answer is 10 years.

Alternate Method

Given: Present age ratio = 4:1. Ratio after 20 years = 2:1.

Formula Used: Ratio & Proportion linear equations.

⇒ Let Daughter's age = x and Man's age = 4x.

⇒ After 20 years: (4x + 20) = 2 × (x + 20)

⇒ 4x + 20 = 2x + 40

⇒ 4x − 2x = 40 − 20

⇒ 2x = 20

⇒ x = 10

∴ The correct answer is 10 years.

📌 Additional Information

Concept of Age Difference

The age difference between two people always remains constant throughout their lives. This is a key tool in solving ratio-based age problems.

Ratio Balancing

To solve age ratios quickly, multiply the ratios so that the difference between the parts (Man − Daughter) is identical in both current and future ratios.

Linear Equations in Ages

If "n" years ago is mentioned, subtract n from current age; if "n" years hence/later is mentioned, add n to the current age.

Question No. 26General Awareness (Q.1)
Expansionary fiscal policy is NOT characterised by:
A)
Decrease in personal taxes
B)
Increase in government spending
C)
Decrease in government spending  ✓ (Correct Answer)
D)
Decrease in business taxes
Correct Option: C) Decrease in government spending
Explanation:

The correct answer is Decrease in government spending.

📌 Key Points

  • Expansionary fiscal policy is used by governments to stimulate economic growth, especially during periods of economic slowdown or recession.
  • It typically involves an increase in government spending on infrastructure, public services, or other sectors to boost aggregate demand. Hence, option 2 is incorrect.
  • Another key characteristic of expansionary fiscal policy is a decrease in personal taxes, which increases disposable income for households, thereby encouraging higher consumer spending. Hence, option 1 is incorrect.
  • Similarly, a decrease in business taxes is often implemented to incentivize businesses to invest more, thereby promoting economic activity. Hence, option 4 is incorrect.
  • However, a decrease in government spending is contrary to the objectives of expansionary fiscal policy, as it reduces aggregate demand rather than increasing it. Hence, option 3 is the correct answer.

📌 Additional Information

  • Expansionary Fiscal Policy:
    • This policy is implemented by governments to encourage economic activity during times of low growth or economic contraction.
    • It aims to increase aggregate demand by injecting more money into the economy through government spending or tax cuts.
    • Examples: Government spending on infrastructure projects, reducing income taxes, or offering tax rebates.
  • Contractionary Fiscal Policy:
    • This is the opposite of expansionary fiscal policy and is used to control inflation or reduce budget deficits.
    • It involves reducing government spending or increasing taxes to decrease aggregate demand in the economy.
    • Example: Raising income tax rates or cutting public sector budgets.
  • Key Features of Expansionary Fiscal Policy:
    • Increase in Government Spending: Used to create jobs and boost economic activities in sectors like construction, health, and education.
    • Reduction in Taxes: Reducing personal income taxes or corporate taxes to increase disposable income and business investment.
    • Budget Deficits: Expansionary policies often lead to higher fiscal deficits as governments spend more than they earn in revenue.
  • Examples of Expansionary Fiscal Policy:
    • The New Deal policies in the United States during the Great Depression involved large-scale public works projects to stimulate economic growth.
    • In 2020, many governments globally implemented expansionary fiscal policies to counter the economic impact of the COVID-19 pandemic, including stimulus packages and tax deferrals.

📌 Important Points

  • Key Differences Between Fiscal and Monetary Policy:
    • Fiscal Policy: Managed by the government, it involves changes in government spending and taxation to influence the economy.
    • Monetary Policy: Managed by central banks, it involves tools like interest rates and money supply to regulate economic growth and inflation.
  • Types of Taxes in Fiscal Policy:
    • Direct Taxes: Taxes levied directly on individuals or businesses, such as income tax and corporate tax.
    • Indirect Taxes: Taxes levied on goods and services, such as GST or VAT.

📌 Key Points

  • The two resistors of 2 ohms and 4 ohms are connected in parallel. In a parallel circuit, the total resistance is calculated using the formula:
    1/Rtotal = 1/R1 + 1/R2.
  • Substituting the values:
    1/Rtotal = 1/2 + 1/4 = 2/4 + 1/4 = 3/4.
    Thus, Rtotal = 4/3 ohms.
  • The circuit is powered by a 6-volt battery. Using Ohm’s Law (V = IR), the total current can be calculated:
    Itotal = V/Rtotal.
    Substitute the values:
    Itotal = 6 / (4/3) = 6 × 3/4 = 4.5 Amperes.
  • Hence, the total current flowing through the circuit is 4.5 Amperes.
  • Each resistor shares the current based on its resistance. The current through the 2-ohm resistor is:
    I = V/R = 6/2 = 3 Amperes.
    The current through the 4-ohm resistor is:
    I = V/R = 6/4 = 1.5 Amperes.

📌 Shortcut Trick

Total Work = LCM of 6, 12, 18 = 36 units.

Combined efficiency of A, B and C = 36 ÷ 6 = 6 units/day.

Efficiency of C = Total efficiency − (A + B) = 6 − (3 + 2) = 1 unit/day.

Time taken by C alone = Total Work ÷ Efficiency of C = 36 ÷ 1 = 36 days.

∴ The correct answer is 36 days.
