📌 Shortcut Trick

∴ The correct answer is ₹20.

Alternate Method

Given:

Number of pens = 3

Cost of one pen = ₹12

Number of notebooks = 2

Total bill amount = ₹76

Formula Used:

Total Cost = (Number of pens × Price per pen) + (Number of notebooks × Price per notebook)

Calculations:

⇒ Cost of 3 pens = 3 × 12 = ₹36

⇒ Let the cost of one notebook be ₹x

⇒ 36 + 2x = 76

⇒ 2x = 76 − 36

⇒ 2x = 40

⇒ x = 40 ÷ 2

⇒ x = ₹20

∴ The correct answer is ₹20.

Unitary Method

This method involves finding the value of a single unit (unit rate) to find the value of any number of units.

Verification Rule

Always check the result by substituting the values back: (3 × 12) + (2 × 20) = 36 + 40 = 76, which confirms the answer is correct.

Who gave the scarcity definition of economics?
A)
Adam Smith
B)
P.A. Samuelson
C)
Alfred Marshall
D)
Lionel Robbins  ✓ (Correct Answer)
Correct Option: D) Lionel Robbins
Explanation:

The correct answer is Lionel Robbins.

📌 Key Points

  • Lionel Robbins, a renowned British economist, gave the scarcity definition of economics in his famous book, An Essay on the Nature and Significance of Economic Science, published in 1932.
  • According to Robbins, economics is the science which studies human behavior as a relationship between ends (wants) and scarce means which have alternative uses.
  • He emphasized that resources are limited, and human wants are unlimited, creating a condition of scarcity.
  • The scarcity definition highlights the concept of choice and opportunity cost, where individuals must prioritize their needs and decide how to allocate scarce resources efficiently.
  • This definition shifted economics from a study of wealth (as proposed by Adam Smith) to a study of human behavior and decision-making under conditions of scarcity.

📌 Additional Information

  • Adam Smith:
    • Known as the Father of Economics, Adam Smith defined economics as a study of the nature and causes of the wealth of nations.
    • His definition focused on the production and accumulation of wealth and was presented in his book, The Wealth of Nations, published in 1776.
    • His approach is referred to as the Wealth Definition of Economics.
  • P.A. Samuelson:
    • Samuelson provided the growth-oriented definition of economics, emphasizing the dynamic aspects of economic growth and development.
    • He defined economics as the study of how societies use scarce resources to produce valuable commodities and distribute them among different people.
    • He is known for his work in modern economic theory.
  • Alfred Marshall:
    • Marshall defined economics as the study of human welfare and material well-being, focusing on how people earn income and use it to satisfy their wants.
    • His definition is referred to as the Welfare Definition of Economics and was presented in his book, Principles of Economics, published in 1890.
    • Marshall's approach emphasized the importance of studying economics in relation to improving human welfare.
  • Concept of Scarcity:
    • Scarcity occurs when resources are limited and cannot satisfy all human wants.
    • This fundamental concept forms the basis of economic problems, such as allocation, production, and distribution.
    • Scarcity forces individuals and societies to make choices and prioritize resource utilization effectively.

📌 Important Points

  • Key Features of Robbins' Definition:
    • Robbins viewed economics as a positive science (descriptive and neutral) rather than a normative one.
    • It focuses on the study of individual behavior in making choices based on limited resources.
    • Introduces the concept of opportunity cost, where choosing one alternative means forgoing others.
    • Emphasizes the universality of economic problems, applicable to all societies irrespective of their stage of development.
  • Criticism of Robbins' Definition:
    • Critics argue that it overlooks the social and welfare aspects of economics emphasized by Marshall.
    • It reduces economics to a study of scarcity and neglects growth and development.
    • Robbins' approach is sometimes seen as too abstract and lacking practical application in real-world economic problems.

    • Maintains the overall cell structure and integrity.

    • Ribosomes: These are involved in protein synthesis and are not connected to structural functions.
