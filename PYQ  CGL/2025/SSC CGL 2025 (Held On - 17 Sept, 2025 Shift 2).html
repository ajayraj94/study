Question No. 20General Intelligence and Reasoning (Q.20)
In a 40 L solution, the ratio of acid to water is 5:3. How much water must be added to make it 1:1?
A)
5 L
B)
6 L
C)
17 L
D)
10 L  ✓ (Correct Answer)
Correct Option: D) 10 L
Explanation:
📌 Shortcut Trick

Initial Ratio (Acid : Water) = 5 : 3 (Total = 8 units)

Final Ratio (Acid : Water) = 1 : 1. Since acid remains constant, make acid parts equal: 1 : 1 × 5 = 5 : 5.

Change in water units = 5 − 3 = 2 units.

8 units = 40 L ⇒ 1 unit = 5 L.

Water to be added = 2 units × 5 L = 10 L.

∴ The correct answer is 10 L.

Alternate Method

Given:

Total solution = 40 L

Ratio of Acid to Water = 5 : 3

Formula Used:

Quantity of component = (Component ratio / Total ratio) × Total quantity

Calculations:

⇒ Quantity of Acid in 40 L = (5 / (5 + 3)) × 40 = (5 / 8) × 40 = 25 L

⇒ Quantity of Water in 40 L = 40 − 25 = 15 L

⇒ Let the amount of water added be 'x' L.

⇒ New ratio of Acid to Water = 25 / (15 + x) = 1 / 1

⇒ 25 = 15 + x

⇒ x = 25 − 15 = 10 L

∴ The correct answer is 10 L.

📌 Additional Information

Ratio Balancing Method

When one component is added or removed, keep the other component's ratio parts equal to simplify the comparison of the changing component.

Rule of Alligation

Used to find the ratio in which two or more ingredients at their respective prices/concentrations must be mixed to produce a mixture of a desired price/concentration.

Concentration Percentage

Percentage of Acid = (Acid / Total Volume) × 100. In a 1:1 mixture, the concentration of both components is exactly 50%.
