The correct answer is Monitors space debris and protects Indian space assets.

📌 Key Points

  • Space Situational Awareness (SSA) is essential for tracking and monitoring space debris, which poses a threat to operational satellites and other space assets.
  • SSA helps in collision avoidance by predicting potential impacts between operational satellites and debris, ensuring safe operations.
  • It plays a critical role in protecting India's space assets, including satellites used for communication, navigation, weather forecasting, and defense purposes.
  • ISRO's SSA program includes efforts to map space debris and provide real-time updates to mitigate risks associated with space operations.
  • The program contributes to global efforts in maintaining space sustainability, ensuring long-term usability of the space environment.

📌 Additional Information

  • Space Debris:
    • Space debris includes defunct satellites, spent rocket stages, and fragments from collisions or explosions in orbit.
    • As of 2023, there are over 34,000 pieces of debris larger than 10 cm and millions of smaller fragments orbiting Earth.
    • Debris travels at high speeds, making even small fragments capable of causing significant damage.
  • ISRO's SSA Program:
    • Launched as part of ISRO's Directorate of Space Situational Awareness and Management (DSSAM).
    • Includes initiatives like Netra (Network for Space Object Tracking and Analysis), which monitors objects in low-Earth orbit and geostationary orbit.
    • Netra uses ground-based radar and optical telescopes to track space objects and debris.
  • Global Context:
    • SSA programs are part of international efforts to ensure space sustainability.
    • Organizations like NASA, ESA (European Space Agency), and JAXA (Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency) also have active SSA programs.
    • The United Nations Office for Outer Space Affairs (UNOOSA) promotes guidelines to reduce space debris.
  • Challenges in Space Debris Management:
    • Identifying and tracking millions of debris fragments is technically challenging.
    • Developing cost-effective methods for debris removal remains a major hurdle.
    • International collaboration is required to ensure debris mitigation standards are followed universally.

📌 Important Points

  • India's Space Assets:
    • India has a growing number of satellites in orbit, including GSAT series for communication, NavIC satellites for navigation, and IRS series for Earth observation.
    • Protecting these assets is crucial for national security, economic development, and scientific progress.
  • Collision Avoidance Strategies:
    • Satellites can be maneuvered to avoid collisions using propulsion systems.
    • Real-time tracking and prediction are critical for timely decision-making.
  • International Agreements:
    • India is a signatory to treaties like the Outer Space Treaty (1967), which promotes peaceful use of space.
    • Global cooperation is key to addressing space debris and ensuring sustainable exploration.

📌 Key Points

  • The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban (PMAY-U) was launched in 2015 by the Government of India to address the urban housing shortage, especially among the economically weaker sections (EWS), low-income groups (LIG), and middle-income groups (MIG).
  • The scheme includes a component of providing financial assistance to eligible beneficiaries for constructing, purchasing, or enhancing affordable housing units. This is a key approach to achieving the scheme's objectives.
  • The financial assistance is provided through a Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS), under which beneficiaries can avail of interest subsidies on housing loans based on their income categories.
  • Under PMAY-U, the focus is on creating “Housing for All” by 2022, with the target of constructing over 1 crore houses in urban areas.
  • The scheme also encourages public-private partnerships (PPP) and state-level implementation to ensure effective execution and broader reach.

📌 Additional Information

  • Key Components of PMAY-U:
    • In-situ Slum Redevelopment (ISSR): Focuses on using land as a resource to provide housing to slum dwellers with the participation of private developers.
    • Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP): Encourages partnerships with private and public sectors to provide affordable housing for the EWS category.
    • Beneficiary-led Construction (BLC): Provides financial assistance to eligible individuals to construct or enhance their houses on their own land.
    • Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS): Offers interest subsidies on housing loans to EWS, LIG, and MIG beneficiaries.
  • Eligibility Criteria:
    • The applicant must belong to the EWS, LIG, or MIG categories, with annual income ranging from up to ₹3 lakh (EWS) to ₹18 lakh (MIG).
    • The beneficiary should not own a pucca house in their name or any family member’s name.
    • Women, Scheduled Castes (SCs), Scheduled Tribes (STs), and other vulnerable groups are given priority.
  • Achievements:
    • As of 2023, over 1.2 crore houses have been sanctioned under PMAY-U, with significant progress in construction and delivery.
    • The scheme has successfully mobilized private investment and created employment opportunities in the construction sector.
  • Challenges:
    • Land acquisition and high real estate costs in urban areas pose challenges to affordable housing projects.
    • Ensuring the timely delivery of houses and maintaining quality standards remain critical issues.

📌 Important Points

  • Option 1: While rental housing for migrant workers is essential, it is not the primary focus of PMAY-U. Instead, it is covered under schemes like the Affordable Rental Housing Complexes (ARHCs).
  • Option 3: The scheme does not mandate the exclusive use of indigenous building materials. It allows flexibility to ensure cost-effectiveness and sustainability.
  • Option 4: PMAY-U focuses on all urban areas, including Tier-1, Tier-2, and Tier-3 cities, rather than concentrating solely on Tier-1 and Tier-2 cities.

  • Beta Decay is a type of nuclear decay in which a beta particle (electron or positron) is emitted from the nucleus.
  • During beta decay, a neutron in the nucleus is converted into a proton (or vice versa), leading to a change in the atomic number but not the mass number.
  • The mass number remains unchanged because the total number of nucleons (protons + neutrons) stays the same.
  • Beta decay increases or decreases the atomic number by 1, thus transforming the atom into a different element.
  • This process helps unstable nuclei achieve a more stable configuration by adjusting the proton-to-neutron ratio.

📌 Additional Information

  • Alpha Decay:
    • Alpha decay involves the emission of an alpha particle (2 protons and 2 neutrons) from the nucleus.
    • This reduces the atomic number by 2 and the mass number by 4.
    • It occurs in heavy nuclei, such as uranium or radium, to achieve stability.
  • Gamma Decay:
    • Gamma decay involves the emission of gamma rays (high-energy photons) from an excited nucleus.
    • It does not change the atomic number or mass number but releases excess energy from the nucleus.
    • This often accompanies alpha or beta decay.
  • Fission:
    • Nuclear fission is the splitting of a heavy nucleus into two lighter nuclei, accompanied by the release of energy and neutrons.
    • It significantly changes both the atomic and mass numbers of the original nucleus.
    • Fission is commonly used in nuclear reactors and weapons.
  • Key Characteristics of Beta Decay:
    • There are two types: Beta-minus (β⁻) and Beta-plus (β⁺) decay.
    • In β⁻ decay, a neutron is converted into a proton, emitting an electron and an antineutrino.
    • In β⁺ decay, a proton is converted into a neutron, emitting a positron and a neutrino.
    • Beta decay plays a critical role in processes like radioactive decay chains and nuclear reactions in stars.

  • Applications of Beta Decay:
    • Used in medical imaging and treatment, such as in PET scans (Positron Emission Tomography).
