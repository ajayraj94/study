In Carnatic music, a laghu with five beats is known as ____ jaati.

A)
Tishra
B)
Chaturashra
C)
Khanda  ✓ (Correct Answer)
D)
Mishra
Correct Answer: C) Khanda
Explanation:

The Correct answer is Khanda Jaati.

📌 Key Points

  • In Carnatic music, rhythm plays a vital role, and it is structured using various rhythmic cycles called talas.
  • A laghu is a rhythmic unit within a tala, and its beat count is determined by the jaati.
  • The term Khanda Jaati refers to a laghu with five beats.
  • In the Khanda Jaati, the rhythmic pattern consists of five syllables or beats, which can be represented as ta ka ta ki ta.
  • This is one of the five types of jaatis in Carnatic music, which include Tishra (3 beats), Chaturashra (4 beats), Khanda (5 beats), Mishra (7 beats), and Sankeerna (9 beats).
  • The usage of Khanda Jaati in talas adds complexity and richness to the rhythmic structure, showcasing the intricacy of South Indian classical music.
  • Khanda Jaati is often employed in compositions to create distinctive and challenging rhythmic patterns that test the skill of performers.

📌 Additional Information

  • Tishra Jaati
    • Tishra Jaati consists of three beats in the laghu.
    • This jaati is represented as ta ki ta.
    • It is one of the simplest jaatis and is widely used in basic rhythmic compositions in Carnatic music.
  • Chaturashra Jaati
    • Chaturashra Jaati consists of four beats in the laghu.
    • The rhythmic pattern for Chaturashra Jaati is ta ka di mi.
    • This is the most common jaati in Carnatic music and serves as the foundation for many talas.
  • Mishra Jaati
    • Mishra Jaati consists of seven beats in the laghu.
    • The rhythmic syllables are represented as ta ki ta ta ka di mi.
    • This jaati is more intricate and is used to add complexity to compositions.

    • The Krebs cycle, also known as the citric acid cycle, occurs in the mitochondrial matrix.
