  • Bhatkhande divided ragas into ten major thaats (parent scales) to provide a simplified and systematic approach to the study of Hindustani music.
  • He emphasized the importance of written documentation of music, which had previously been passed down orally, ensuring its preservation for future generations.
  • His efforts led to the establishment of music institutions, such as the Marris College of Music (now Bhatkhande Music Institute University) in Lucknow, to formalize music education in India.

  • Meanders are a characteristic feature of mature river systems and develop over time as the river seeks to balance its energy and flow.
