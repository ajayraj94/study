Addition and Subtraction of Surds

Only like surds (surds having the exact same number under the root) can be added or subtracted directly. For example, x√a + y√a = (x + y)√a.

BODMAS Rule

Always follow the order of operations. Roots (Orders) are simplified first, followed by addition and subtraction from left to right as they have the same priority level.
