Find out why white silver chloride turns grey in sunlight.
A)
Due to rusting of silver in presence of oxygen
B)
Due to redox reaction
C)
Due to the decomposition of silver chloride into silver and chlorine by light  ✓ (Correct Answer)
D)
Due to the displacement of silver chloride to silver oxide
Correct Answer: C) Due to the decomposition of silver chloride into silver and chlorine by light
Explanation:

The correct answer is Due to the decomposition of silver chloride into silver and chlorine by light.

📌 Key Points

  • Silver chloride (AgCl) is a white crystalline solid.
  • When exposed to sunlight, AgCl decomposes into silver (Ag) and chlorine gas (Cl2).
  • The silver formed is a grey metal, which imparts the grey color to the white AgCl.
  • This reaction is a type of photodecomposition reaction, where light energy causes the chemical breakdown of a compound.

📌 Additional Information

  • Photodecomposition Reaction
    • This is a chemical reaction in which a compound is broken down into simpler substances by the action of light.
    • In the case of silver chloride, light energy breaks the bond between silver and chlorine atoms.
    • Such reactions are common in photochemistry and are used in various applications including photography.
  • Silver Chloride (AgCl)
    • It is a white crystalline solid that is well known for its low solubility in water.
    • AgCl is used in photographic films and papers, where it plays a crucial role in capturing images.
  • Photographic Process
    • Silver halides like AgCl are used in photographic films because they are sensitive to light.
    • When exposed to light, they decompose to form silver, which creates the image on the film.
    • This process is the basis for traditional film photography.

    • M1: This is the narrow measure of money supply, which includes currency in circulation and demand deposits (checking accounts).

    • M3: This includes M2 plus large time deposits, institutional money market funds, and other larger liquid assets.
  • Importance of Money Supply
    • The money supply is a critical factor in determining inflation levels, interest rates, and overall economic stability.
    • Central banks, such as the Federal Reserve or the Reserve Bank of India, use measures of the money supply to implement monetary policy.
    • Changes in the money supply can affect the economy's performance, influencing everything from consumer spending to business investment.

B)

D)
AC = PR and ∠P = 65°
Correct Answer: B) PQ = 7 cm and ∠R = 75°
Explanation:

📌 Shortcut Trick

For congruent triangles ΔABC ≅ ΔQPR, the vertices map to each other in the exact order written.

Mappings: A → Q, B → P, C → R.

Side PQ maps directly to AB, so PQ = AB = 7 cm.

Angle R maps directly to angle C, so ∠R = ∠C = 75°.

This matches the given condition perfectly without calculating any other unknown sides or angles.

∴ The correct answer is PQ = 7 cm and ∠R = 75°.

Alternate Method

Given:

⇒ ΔABC ≅ ΔQPR

⇒ AB = 7 cm, AC = 8 cm

⇒ ∠B = 65°, ∠C = 75°

Concept Used:

⇒ CPCT (Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles): If two triangles are congruent, their corresponding angles and sides are precisely equal.

Calculations:

⇒ Since ΔABC ≅ ΔQPR, the exact correspondence is given by A ↔ Q, B ↔ P, and C ↔ R.

⇒ Corresponding sides match: AB = QP, BC = PR, and AC = QR

⇒ Corresponding angles match: ∠A = ∠Q, ∠B = ∠P, and ∠C = ∠R

⇒ Applying the given dimensions directly to the mapped parts:

⇒ PQ = AB = 7 cm

⇒ QR = AC = 8 cm

⇒ ∠P = ∠B = 65°

⇒ ∠R = ∠C = 75°

⇒ Also, by the angle sum property, ∠A = 180° − (65° + 75°) = 40°, meaning ∠Q = 40°.

⇒ Comparing our derived values with the given options, we find that PQ = 7 cm and ∠R = 75° is the only correct statement.

∴ The correct answer is PQ = 7 cm and ∠R = 75°.

📌 Additional Information

Congruence Criteria

Two triangles are strictly congruent if they satisfy SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, or RHS congruency conditions.

CPCT Theorem

CPCT denotes 'Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles'. It establishes that if two triangles are congruent, all their equivalent angles and sides are identical.

Angle Sum Property

The total sum of interior angles in any triangle is exactly 180°. This helps determine the third angle if two are known (∠A = 180° − ∠B − ∠C).

Similar vs Congruent

Congruent triangles have an identical shape and exact size, whereas similar triangles possess the identical shape but may differ proportionally in size.
