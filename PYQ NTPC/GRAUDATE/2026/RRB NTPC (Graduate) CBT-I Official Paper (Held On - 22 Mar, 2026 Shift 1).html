The abbreviation ASCII is a critical computer standard used worldwide. The letter C in ASCII stands for Code, indicating that ASCII is a standard system for assigning a unique numerical value to every single character, which is known as a character _________________.

Correct Option: C) encoding scheme
Explanation:

The correct answer is encoding scheme.

📌 Key Points

  • ASCII stands for American Standard Code for Information Interchange, which is a character encoding scheme used to represent text in computers and other communication devices.
  • The letter C in the acronym stands for Code, signifying that it is a systematic set of rules used to convert information, such as letters or symbols, into a numerical value that a computer can process. Hence, the statement regarding ASCII being a character encoding scheme is correct.
  • It was first published as a standard in 1963 by the American Standards Association (now known as ANSI - American National Standards Institute).
  • The Standard ASCII system uses 7 bits to represent each character, which allows for a total of 128 unique characters (ranging from 0 to 127).
  • These characters include uppercase letters (A-Z), lowercase letters (a-z), digits (0-9), punctuation marks, and various control characters.

📌 Additional Information

  • Character Encoding: This is the process of assigning binary values to characters so that electronic devices can store and transmit data. Without a universal encoding scheme, different computer systems would be unable to communicate effectively.
  • Extended ASCII: While the standard version uses 7 bits, Extended ASCII uses 8 bits (one byte). This expansion allows for 256 characters, accommodating special characters, mathematical symbols, and characters from different languages.
  • Unicode and UTF-8: In modern computing, ASCII has been largely superseded by Unicode, specifically the UTF-8 encoding. UTF-8 is backward compatible with ASCII, meaning the first 128 characters in Unicode are identical to those in the ASCII set.
  • Data Representation: Each ASCII character corresponds to a specific decimal and hexadecimal value. For example, the uppercase letter 'A' is represented by the decimal number 65, while the lowercase 'a' is 97, and the digit '0' is 48.

📌 Important Points

  • Control Characters: The first 32 characters (0–31) in the ASCII table are designated as control characters. These are non-printing characters used to control hardware devices like printers and teletypewriters (e.g., Line Feed, Carriage Return, and Escape).
  • Applications: ASCII is used in programming, data protocols, and plain text files. Even though complex documents use Unicode, ASCII remains the fundamental building block for source code in languages like C, Java, and Python.
  • History: Before ASCII, there was no standard way for different mainframe computers to talk to each other. The introduction of this standard in the United States revolutionized the IT industry by ensuring interoperability across different manufacturers.
  • Numerical Precision: In 7-bit ASCII, the binary representation of 'A' is 01000001 (using an 8-bit byte where the first bit is 0). This consistency allows network protocols to ensure that data sent from one location arrives exactly as intended at the destination.

  • Under Article 243ZD of the Constitution, it is mandatory for every state to constitute a District Planning Committee (DPC) at the district level to consolidate the plans prepared by the Panchayats and the Municipalities in the district and to prepare a draft development plan for the district as a whole.   • This amendment added a new Part IX-A to the Constitution of India, titled 'The Municipalities', consisting of provisions from Articles 243-P to 243-ZG.
  • The Act also added the 12th Schedule to the Constitution, which contains 18 functional items that fall under the purview of municipalities, such as urban planning, regulation of land use, and water supply.

📌 Additional Information

  • 73rd Amendment Act (1992):
    • This amendment granted constitutional status to Panchayati Raj Institutions (PRIs) for rural local self-government.
    • It added Part IX and the 11th Schedule to the Constitution, covering 29 functional items for Panchayats.
    • While it deals with District Planning via integration, the specific mandate for Metropolitan Planning Committees is unique to the 74th Amendment.
  • 97th Amendment Act (2011):
    • This amendment gave constitutional status and protection to Co-operative Societies.
    • It inserted Part IX-B into the Constitution and made the right to form co-operative societies a Fundamental Right under Article 19.
    • It also added a new Directive Principle of State Policy (Article 43-B) regarding the promotion of co-operative societies.

    • This amendment provided constitutional status to the National Commission for Backward Classes (NCBC).

    • The NCBC was previously a statutory body under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment.

  • Types of Municipalities: The Act provides for three types of municipalities: Nagar Panchayat for transitional areas, Municipal Council for smaller urban areas, and Municipal Corporation for larger urban areas.

Let C be a circle with centre O and AB be a minor arc on C. Let S be a point on the minor arc AB. If AB subtends an angle 120&deg; at O, then find the measure of &ang;ASB.
A)

B)
120&deg;  ✓ (Correct Answer)
C)
100&deg;
D)
60&deg;
Correct Option: B) 120&deg;
Explanation:
📌 Shortcut Trick

Angle on the minor arc = 180° − (Central angle subtended by the same arc ÷ 2)

Given ∠AOB = 120°

∠ASB = 180° − (120° ÷ 2)

∠ASB = 180° − 60° = 120°

∴ The correct answer is 120°.

Alternate Method

Given: Centre O, Minor arc AB subtends ∠AOB = 120° at O. S is on the minor arc AB.

Formula: Angle at the major arc = 1/2 × Central angle; Sum of opposite angles in a cyclic quadrilateral = 180°.

⇒ Let P be any point on the major arc AB.

⇒ Angle subtended by minor arc AB at major arc (∠APB) = 1/2 × ∠AOB

⇒ ∠APB = 1/2 × 120° = 60°

⇒ Since points A, S, B, P lie on the circle, ASBP is a cyclic quadrilateral.

⇒ In cyclic quadrilateral ASBP, ∠ASB + ∠APB = 180°

⇒ ∠ASB + 60° = 180°

⇒ ∠ASB = 180° − 60° = 120°

∴ The correct answer is 120°.

📌 Additional Information

Central Angle Theorem

The angle subtended by an arc at the center of a circle is double the angle subtended by the same arc at any point on the remaining part of the circle.

Cyclic Quadrilateral Property

A quadrilateral is cyclic if its vertices lie on a circle. The sum of its opposite angles is always 180° (∠A + ∠C = 180° and ∠B + ∠D = 180°).

Angle in Minor and Major Arcs

If a minor arc subtends θ at the center, the angle at the major arc is θ/2, and the angle at the minor arc is (360° − θ)/2 or 180° − θ/2.

  • The three primary types of planetary winds are the Trade Winds, the Westerlies, and the Polar Easterlies.
  • Trade Winds blow from the Sub-tropical High Pressure belt (around 30° N and S) towards the Equatorial Low Pressure belt. Because of the Coriolis Force, they blow from the North-East in the Northern Hemisphere and the South-East in the Southern Hemisphere.
  • Westerlies blow from the Sub-tropical High Pressure belts towards the Sub-polar Low Pressure belts (around 60° N and S). They are more vigorous in the Southern Hemisphere due to the vast expanse of ocean.

The correct answer is IPC (Instructions Per Cycle).

📌 Key Points

  • IPC (Instructions Per Cycle) is a performance metric that represents the average number of machine instructions a Central Processing Unit (CPU) can execute in a single clock cycle. It serves as a primary measure of a processor's microarchitecture efficiency. Hence, statement D is correct.
  • The IPC of a CPU is not a fixed number; it varies based on the type of software code being run and the complexity of the instructions. Tasks involving simple integer math typically yield a higher IPC than complex floating-point operations.
  • Modern high-performance CPUs utilize superscalar architecture, which allows them to execute more than one instruction per cycle by having multiple execution units (like ALUs) working in parallel.
  • Factors that influence IPC include the size of the cache (L1, L2, and L3), the efficiency of the branch predictor, and the pipeline depth. A deeper pipeline may allow for higher clock speeds but can sometimes lower the IPC due to the penalty of branch mispredictions.

📌 Additional Information

  • Clock Speed: Measured in Gigahertz (GHz) or Megahertz (MHz), it indicates the number of pulses or cycles generated by an oscillator per second. While it tells us how fast the CPU "ticks," it does not reveal how much work is done during each tick. Hence, statement A is incorrect.
  • Bus Speed: This refers to the frequency at which the Front Side Bus (FSB) or modern interconnects (like Intel's QPI or AMD's Infinity Fabric) communicate between the CPU and the Northbridge or System Memory (RAM). It determines the bandwidth available for data transfer rather than instruction execution. Hence, statement B is incorrect.
  • Core Count: This is the number of independent processing cores integrated into a single computing component. While having more cores allows a computer to handle more threads simultaneously (parallelism), it is an aggregate measure of capacity rather than a per-cycle efficiency metric. Hence, statement C is incorrect.
  • The Performance Formula: The overall speed of a CPU is determined by the product of Clock Speed and IPC. This explains why a modern 3.0 GHz processor can outperform an older 4.0 GHz processor from a previous decade; the newer architecture has a much higher IPC.

📌 Important Points

  • Historical Context: In the late 1990s and early 2000s, manufacturers primarily focused on clock speed (the Megahertz myth).   • MIPS vs IPC: MIPS (Millions of Instructions Per Second) is another way to measure CPU speed, but it is considered less reliable than IPC because it does not account for the complexity of different instruction sets (like RISC vs CISC).
  • Bottlenecks: Even if a CPU has a high theoretical IPC, it can be limited by memory latency. If the data is not in the L1 cache, the CPU must wait hundreds of cycles to fetch it from RAM, causing the effective IPC to drop.
